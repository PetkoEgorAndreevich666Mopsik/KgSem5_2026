Environment: Papermill, from the Khronos glTF Sample Environments collection.
https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Environments
Original panorama: http://www.hdrlabs.com/sibl/archive/

irradiance.dds: precomputed Lambertian convolution, RGBA16F cubemap.
prefilter.dds: precomputed GGX convolution, RGBA16F cubemap, 11 mip levels.
The source KTX2 files are retained; tools/convert_ibl.py converts their layout to DDS without changing values.
brdf.dds: RG16F split-sum BRDF integration, 256 x 256, 1024 Hammersley samples per texel.
Regenerate with tools/integrate_brdf.py (Python + NumPy).

Implementation references:
https://learnopengl.com/PBR/Lighting
https://learnopengl.com/PBR/IBL/Specular-IBL
https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-IBL-Sampler
